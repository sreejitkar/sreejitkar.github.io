Paxos Made Simple

Lamport's distilled deconstruction of the Synod protocol and state machine replication. Shows that the consensus algorithm is straightforward when derived purely from invariant deductions over quorum intersections.

Re-engineers distributed consensus explicitly for human understandability by decomposing the problem into discrete modules: randomized leader election, log replication, and safety invariants.

Consensus ★ Landmark
Communications of the ACM · 1978

Time, Clocks, and the Ordering of Events in a Distributed System

Leslie Lamport (Massachusetts Computer Associates)

The seminal foundation of modern distributed systems theory. Introduces logical clocks, the "happens-before" partial ordering relationship (→), and total event ordering in asynchronous networks without physical clocks.

#logical-clocks #happens-before #partial-order

Conflict-Free Replicated Data Types (CRDTs)

Marc Shapiro, Nuno Preguiça, Carlos Baquero, Marek Zawirski (INRIA & LIP6)

Formalizes convergent (CvRDT) and commutative (CmRDT) replicated data structures. Proves that mathematical lattice joins guarantee strong eventual consistency across partitions without coordination bottlenecks.

#crdt #eventual-consistency #lattice-joins

ACM SOSP · 2007

Dynamo: Amazon's Highly Available Key-Value Store

Giuseppe DeCandia, Deniz Hastorun, Madan Jampani, et al. (Amazon.com)

Masterclass in engineering for 99.9th percentile SLA latency. Sacrifices strict consistency for high availability using consistent hashing with virtual nodes, vector clocks, sloppy quorums, hinted handoffs, and Merkle tree anti-entropy.

#dynamo #consistent-hashing #vector-clocks

Bigtable: A Distributed Storage System for Structured Data

Fay Chang, Jeffrey Dean, Sanjay Ghemawat, Wilson C. Hsieh, et al. (Google)

Introduced the sparse, distributed, persistent multidimensional sorted map. Solidified the core LSM-tree architecture (MemTable, immutable SSTables, Bloom filters, and compaction) that underpins LevelDB, RocksDB, and Cassandra.

#bigtable #lsm-tree #sstable

Pioneered modern distributed storage design by accepting commodity component failures as constant events. Optimized for large append-only data streams, single-master control plane metadata, and autonomous chunkserver replication.
